AI engine

AI reads the document and leaves the decisions to you.

Quickscan is the AI layer shared by Invoices and Orders. It reads any layout, scores what it extracts, and sends uncertain fields to a person instead of guessing.

  • Quickscan extraction

    AI reads the vendor or customer, document number, dates, purchase or sales order, line items, tax and totals straight from the PDF text layer.

  • Confidence on the document

    Each document carries a confidence score and every line shows what the model read. Low scores surface for review before anyone approves.

  • Correction in the loop

    Every field and line is editable. A correction re-runs validation, matching and coding, and resets the approval so nothing slips through.

  • Optional model, local fallback

    Connect an AI model with your own key. Without one, the local parser reads the same text layer. Only extracted text is sent, never the PDF.

Invoices: every supplier invoice matched, coded and approved before it reaches your desk.

The Invoices workflow uses AI quickscan to turn a supplier PDF into an unposted purchase invoice. It reads the document, validates it, matches every line to the purchase order and receipt, applies your tolerances, codes the GL, and routes only the exceptions.

  • Quickscan extraction

    AI reads the text layer and returns the vendor, lines and totals. No vendor template to build or maintain, and a new supplier is created automatically when no match exists.

  • Three-way match with tolerances

    Unit price, line amount, order total, shipping and quantity. Accept a variance with a reason, or send it back.

  • Approval rules

    Rules run in priority order. Total over 2,500. Expense over 1,000. Vendor name like Freight. The first match wins.

  • Exception queues

    Validation, duplicate, PO mismatch, goods not received, system hold and GL coding each get a status and an owner in the exception queue.

  • ERP export

    Unposted invoices carry line detail, GL accounts and receipt references. The PDF, corrections and approvals stay on the record.

Orders: every customer order turned into a clean ERP order.

The Orders workflow uses AI quickscan to read the order PDF, matches it to the sales order and your live inventory, maps each line to a product, applies approval rules, and exports an unposted ERP order. No retyping between the inbox and the order book.

  • Inventory checks

    Every line is checked against available inventory. Insufficient stock holds the order before it becomes a promise you cannot keep.

  • Product mapping

    Unmapped lines block export until someone maps them, so an order never posts against the wrong SKU.

  • Sales order match

    The PDF is checked against the ERP sales order with tolerances on price, quantity, shipping and totals. A mismatch is reported and kept in the queue.

  • Customer records

    No customer record yet? The workflow creates one and routes the order through the same approval engine as every other order.

  • Approval rules

    Amounts, order types and customer names route to approver groups with limits. Every action is recorded.
